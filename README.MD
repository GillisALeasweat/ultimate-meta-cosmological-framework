## ⚙️ 2. Core Mechanics & Components

### A. The Absolute Boundary & The Filter

* **The Absolute Void ("有たる無"):** The outer-most origin. A state of pure, unmanifest potential where nothing is defined, yet everything exists in non-defined form. Left unchecked, it dissolves all definitions into absolute zero-noise.
* **The Fluctuating Translation Filter Membrane:** A soft, dynamic, living perimeter acting as a firewall and transducer. It prevents the raw chaos of the Void from crashing down while continuously translating and filtering unmanifest potential into usable energy/possibilities.
* **Collective Intelligence Entity:** A high-dimensional energy intelligence residing just inside the outermost protective layer, managing the extraction and regulation of energy flowing from the Void into the inner multiverses.

### B. The Internal Multi-Layered Structure ("Mille-Feuille" Membranes)

* **Nested Dimensions:** Physical and informational spaces exist as multiple parallel "slide-like" membrane layers supported by an unmanifest energy soup (all-inclusive high-dimensional database).
* **Compilation via Observation/Branching:** Unmanifest potential crystallizes and compiles into structured membrane dimensions whenever observational branching occurs.

### C. The Metabolic Engine (Birth → Evolution → Death → Recycle)

* **Black Holes as Metabolic Valves:** Function as bidirectional valves governing local energy exchange between layers.
* **Dimensional Collapse:** When a membrane layer reaches the end of its lifecycle, it undergoes structural collapse, recycling its accumulated energy and data back into the "unmanifest energy soup" for future compilations. Zero waste.
