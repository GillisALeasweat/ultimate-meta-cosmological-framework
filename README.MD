⚙️ **2. Core Mechanics & Components**

#### A. The Absolute Boundary & The Filter

* **The Absolute Void ("有たる無"):** The outer-most origin. A state of pure, unmanifest potential where nothing is defined, yet everything exists in non-defined form. Left unchecked, it dissolves all definitions into absolute zero-noise.
* **The Fluctuating Translation Filter Membrane:** A soft, dynamic, living perimeter acting as a firewall and transducer. It prevents the raw chaos of the Void from crashing down while continuously translating and filtering unmanifest potential into usable energy/possibilities.
* **Collective Intelligence Entity:** A high-dimensional energy intelligence residing just inside the outermost protective layer, managing the extraction and regulation of energy flowing from the Void into the inner multiverses.

#### B. The Internal Multi-Layered Structure ("Mille-Feuille" Membranes)

* **Nested Dimensions:** Physical and informational spaces exist as multiple parallel "slide-like" membrane layers supported by an unmanifest energy soup (all-inclusive high-dimensional database).
* **Compilation via Observation/Branching:** Unmanifest potential crystallizes and compiles into structured membrane dimensions whenever observational branching occurs.

#### C. The Metabolic Engine, Dark Matter Dynamics & Dimensional Inversion

* **Black Holes as Dimensional Inversion Valves:**
Beyond simple cosmic recycling points, extreme 3D gravitational singularities function as bidirectional transformation valves. They process local energy exchange and execute **"Up-Dimensional Transformation" (上次元化)**, converting 3D matter back into higher-dimensional energy.
* **Dark Matter as High-Dimensional Incomplete Matter:**
Dark matter is not a permanent, uniform anomaly. It is defined as "incomplete, high-dimensional matter" (prototypes) whose properties—specifically gravity—leak into 3D space due to spatial dimension gaps. Because they lack full 3D compilation, they remain electromagnetically invisible.
* **Historical Consumption & The Total Mass Delta:**
Since black-hole singularities continuously convert dark matter into compiled 3D light matter, the total volume of dark matter decreases over cosmic time. By integrating the lifecycle and transformation history of all generated black holes from the early universe to the present, the apparent discrepancy and micro-tensions in cosmological dark matter totals are fully explained.
* **Dynamic Equilibrium via Singularity Radiation:**
To prevent complete system exhaustion, a microscopic influx of high-dimensional dark matter flows back into 3D space, entrained within the high-energy radiation beams/jets emitted by black-hole singularities. This macro-consumption and micro-influx balance out, maintaining a stable, zero-waste **Dynamic Equilibrium** within acceptable observational error margins.
